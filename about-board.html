Founders & Board

Founders who shaped Mridulashray’s ethos of service.

Mridulashray was established by physicians, administrators, advocates, and philanthropists who wanted dignity-first healthcare, education, and animal welfare in Rewari. Their collective experience still guides every voluntary clinic, Gau Shaala upgrade, and women’s skills lab we operate today.

  • Founding decadeGrassroots programmes running since 2014 with zero interruptions.
  • Cross-sectorHealthcare, governance, law, and community expertise at one table.
  • Hands-onFounders personally review clinics, nurseries, and beneficiary audits.
